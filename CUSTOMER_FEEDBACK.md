# What customers have been telling us

**From:** Head of Talent, fintech, about 60 people
**Subject:** Shortlisting
**Date:** 12 Jun

Hi,

Some feedback from me, since I'm in the results page most days at the
moment. We've got three roles open and results are coming in through the
week, and there are three of us reviewing them: me, the hiring manager for
each role, and occasionally our COO when she has a spare ten minutes.

The problem is that the review happens in CultureTest but the decision lives
in a spreadsheet. I go through the list, decide who I want to take to
interview, then type their names into a Google Sheet so the others can see
who I've flagged. Then one of them does the same thing and now we've got two
lists. Last week we double-booked someone for a first interview because two
of us had shortlisted him separately.

What I actually want is for the shortlist to just live in CultureTest. I tick
someone, it stays ticked, the other two can see it, and when I come back on
Thursday I can pick up where I left off rather than reconstructing it from
the sheet. Ideally I can see just the shortlisted people without scrolling
through everyone again.

There is a checkbox on each row already, which I assumed did this, but it
doesn't seem to remember anything.

Thanks,
Rachel

**From:** Head of Operations, Brightline Recruitment, agency with three offices
**Subject:** Re: rollout to Manchester and Leeds
**Date:** 21 Jun

Hi Calum,

Following up on our call. Before I put this in front of the other two
offices I want to be honest about where we are with it, because the London
team have been using it for four months now and there are a few things that
will get worse rather than better with more people on it.

Volume is the big one. We've got about 400 candidates across three live roles
at the moment, and the results page takes long enough to come up that the
team have just learned to wait. When they're hunting for a particular person
they'll change the role filter, then the score filter, then type a name, and
it doesn't always feel like it keeps up. Sometimes what they're looking at
doesn't seem to match what they've picked and they have to change something
again to get it to update.

The other thing is sharing. I'll filter down to, say, Engineering above 75
and send the link to a consultant in Manchester, and what she gets is the
full unfiltered list. So we end up sending screenshots, which is not great
for a tool we're paying for.

For the rollout itself: each office needs to see only its own candidates,
but I'd like one login rather than three. The Manchester and Leeds managers
will each need their own access too.

We're reviewing all our tools ahead of renewal in August, so it would be
good to know what's realistic between now and then. One of the consultants
also asked whether there's a dark mode and whether it can push results into
Greenhouse, but those are nice-to-haves.

Best,
Dana

**From:** Hiring Manager, retailer, about 200 people
**Subject:** Has a result been lost?
**Date:** 26 Jun

Hi Calum,

I've had a bit of a scare this morning and I want to understand what
happened.

I opened the results page to prepare for a panel on Monday and it told me no
candidates matched my filters. I hadn't changed the filters, and I know for
certain that Priya Sharma completed her assessment because she forwarded me
the confirmation email. For about ten minutes I thought her result had been
lost and I was drafting an email asking her to do it again. Then I refreshed
and she was there, along with everyone else.

This is the second time this has happened to me this month. The first time I
assumed I'd done something wrong. I now don't trust that an empty list means
what it says, which is a problem when I'm using it to decide who gets an
interview.

While I'm writing: the top of the page tells me there are 4 strong fits, and
when I count the green labels in the table there are 5. Which is right? I put
these numbers in a weekly report to our CEO and I'd rather not be guessing.
The average score at the top also says "NaN", which I assume is a bug.

One more thing while I'm here. I'm retyping every score into a spreadsheet
for the hiring committee, because that's what they want to see. If there's a
way to export the list, even just a CSV, that would save me an hour a week.

Thanks,
Tom

## Context you'd have if you worked here

- The backend is Go and Postgres. The API spec is shared between frontend
  and backend, so if the right answer is "the backend should do this", say
  so. That's a normal conversation here.
- We're a small team and we release when features are finished. We'd rather have something
  that works now and is clear about what it doesn't do than something
  complete in six weeks.
- People make hiring decisions from this screen. If it shows something
  wrong, a real person gets rejected or put forward based on bad
  information.
